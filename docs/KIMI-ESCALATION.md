## Safeguards and limits

The existing cloud ledger and HTTPS transport are reused, with optional pricing
callbacks and a separate current-pilot cap; old MiMo settings are unchanged.
Historical ledgers are reconciled by charge identity rather than adding inherited
totals repeatedly. Authenticated remaining allowance is checked before every
reservation, accounting for other-application spending observed at that time.
Concurrent outside-app spending cannot be predicted; the unchanged provider-side
key limit remains the outer restriction.

Reservations are durable before dispatch. Disconnects retain their maximum
charge; restart never turns a reservation into a new attempt. Unknown costs block
further spending until reconciled. Reported costs, reasoning usage, readiness,
failures and any authorised work share accounting. Identity mismatch persists a
halt. A failed first qualification does not permit later tasks on resume.

Reviews reuse exact draft-hash and scope/anchor validation. Stale, ambiguous,
overlapping, destructive or out-of-scope edits cannot silently apply. Applied
claims, rejected edits, suggestions and uncertainty are recorded separately;
the local baseline stays unchanged. Compilation uses the existing isolated image
and is a separate status. Neither a build nor model agreement establishes
mathematical fidelity. No whole-document rewrite fallback, paid repair loop,
web/search tool, cloud OCR or automatic local-to-cloud escalation is enabled.

with this adapter only after the local trial reached `focused_trial_finished`
